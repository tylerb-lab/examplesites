About the studio

Architecture
as practice.

Form Studio is a Sydney-based architecture and interior design practice founded in 2012. We work on residential and commercial projects, with a focus on buildings that are quiet, considered and built to last.

We believe good architecture doesn't need to announce itself. The best rooms are the ones you don't notice — you just feel at ease in them.
