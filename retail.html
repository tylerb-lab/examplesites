Our story

Objects made
to last.

Forage Supply Co. started as a search for things made slowly, by people who care. We stock Australian and international makers who share our commitment to materials that age well and craftsmanship that holds up.

Our shop is in Surry Hills, Sydney — open Tuesday to Sunday. We ship Australia-wide, and to New Zealand.

Made to last

No fast homewares. Everything we stock is built for years of use.

Considered sourcing

We visit makers where possible and know the story behind what we sell.

Sustainable materials

Natural fibres, recycled materials, minimal plastic packaging.

Local first

Majority Australian-made, always. Supporting local craft where we can.
