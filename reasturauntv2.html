Our story

Food that
tastes like somewhere.

Ember opened in 2019 with a simple premise: get the best produce you can, put it over fire, and don't overthink it. Our kitchen runs on two custom wood-fired ovens.

Head chef Marcus Ellery trained in Adelaide and spent five years in regional Spain. His food is direct and unfussy — good ingredients, live fire, smart technique.
