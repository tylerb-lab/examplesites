Services

From design
to maintenance.

Landscape Design

Full design service from site analysis to planting plans and construction documentation. Hand-drawn or CAD drawings.

Garden Construction

Paving, decking, retaining walls, drainage, irrigation and planting. We build what we design — or work from your plans.

Planting & Turf

Plant selection, sourcing and installation. Native gardens, edible gardens, cottage planting and lawns. We know what survives Sydney summers.

Regular Maintenance

Fortnightly or monthly maintenance programs. Mowing, pruning, weeding, mulching and seasonal care to keep your garden at its best.

Irrigation Systems

Automated irrigation design and installation. Drip systems, pop-up sprinklers and smart controllers. Set it and forget it.

Commercial Landscaping

Body corporates, office buildings, retail and hospitality. Ongoing maintenance contracts and one-off upgrades.
