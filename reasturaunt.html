About

Honest food,
good company.

Ember Kitchen opened in 2019 as a love letter to wood-fire cooking. Our kitchen is built around two custom wood-fired ovens and a whole-animal ethos — we use everything, waste nothing.

Chef Marcus Ellery trained in Adelaide and spent five years cooking in regional Spain before returning to Sydney. His food is direct and unfussy — good ingredients, live fire, smart technique.

We're a neighbourhood restaurant. We want you to come back. That means consistent quality, a menu that changes with the seasons, and staff who actually enjoy being here.
