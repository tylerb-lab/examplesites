About the park

More than just a
campsite.

Riverside Valley Holiday Park has been welcoming families, couples and solo adventurers to the Hawkesbury for over 40 years. Set across 12 hectares of river flat and bushland, we're a proper park — not a car park with grass.

Whether you're pulling in with a caravan, pitching a tent under the stars, or settling into one of our self-contained cabins, you'll find yourself genuinely away from it all — while still being just an hour from Sydney.
