About

Hi, I'm Clara.

I've been photographing weddings for over a decade. I shoot with a documentary eye — quietly, unobtrusively, catching the moments that happen when you're not posing.

I believe the best wedding photos look like your wedding actually looked. Not staged. Not filtered into something unrecognisable. Just you, in the light that was there, in the moment it happened.

I'm based in Sydney but travel throughout Australia and internationally. My calendar fills quickly — if you're planning a wedding in the next 18 months, get in touch early.
